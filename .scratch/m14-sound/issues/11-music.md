# 11 — Music: Lobby and Round

**What to build:** music on the music bus, in the Lobby and during a Round.
**ADR 0087.**

**Blocked by:** 02, 03

**Status:** done on tests (2026-09-17). Hearing it, and the music level, is the user's check.

## How it behaves after

- **Playlists** (user, 2026-09-17): the Lobby shuffles its tracks and the Round
  (COUNTDOWN and RUNNING) shuffles its own. The same track never plays twice in
  a row, and the two playlists crossfade on the phase change.
- The tracks are generated by the game's author (Suno, Stable Audio, ADR 0087
  amendment): Lobby `music/lobby_*`, Round `music/round_*`.
- ROUND_END and RESULTS duck the music under their jingles (10).
- Music streams through an `<audio>` element fed into the music bus (a
  `MediaElementAudioSourceNode`), never decoded whole.
- The music keeps playing across Screens that don't unmount the game, and fades
  out when leaving the Match.
  *Superseded (user, 2026-09-17):* the Lobby's playlist is the **app's** music. It plays on every
  React Screen and in free roam, from the first gesture, and carries on across navigation. Only a
  Match's Round switches to the Round's playlist. Leaving a Match crossfades back to the Lobby's.
- MUSIC at 0 pauses the element instead of playing it silently.

## As built

- **Where the player lives:** there is no game module in the Lobby. `/lobby` renders the Lobby Screen
  on the shell-owned socket and only mounts `<GameCanvas>` once the phase leaves LOBBY (ADR 0056).
  So the player is not on the Stage's engine. It lives on the page:
  - **`audio/sharedContext.ts`:** a three-free page `AudioContext`. `gameAudio.ts` now hands this
    same context to three.js (`AudioContext.setContext`), so music and the Stage still share one
    graph, and the menu bundle doesn't pull three.
  - **`audio/music.ts`:**
    - `MusicPlayer` has its own chain: two element decks → duck gain → volume gain → speakers. It
      applies MASTER × MUSIC itself (`musicGain` in `lib/audioSettings.ts`), since it outlives every
      Stage. The Stage's music bus stays unfed. ADR 0087 is amended to say so.
    - `musicPlayer()` is the page's one player.
- **Behaviour:**
  - **Playlists:** `musicFor(phase)` gives lobby for LOBBY, round for COUNTDOWN/RUNNING, and round
    ducked to 0.35 for ROUND_END/RESULTS.
  - **Shuffle:** `nextTrack` never repeats the last track. A track that ends is followed by another
    from its playlist.
  - **Phase change:** the idle deck loads the new playlist and fades in, while the other fades out
    (1.5 s) and pauses once quiet. A job counter makes a stale pause harmless.
  - **Volume at 0:** both elements pause. Raising it again plays the active deck.
  - **Autoplay refusal:** swallowed. `resume()` retries it.
  - **Leaving:** `stop()` fades out over 1.2 s and pauses.
- **Wiring:**
  - **App-wide since the user's correction (2026-09-17):**
    - `startAppMusic(window)` in `main.tsx` starts the Lobby's playlist on the first gesture,
      unless a Match already chose what plays. Later gestures resume the context and retry a
      refused play.
    - `musicPlayer()` applies the stored MASTER × MUSIC when it is created and follows every
      change.
    - `setMusicPhase(phase | null)` is the only way in. `null` means outside any Match, which is
      the Lobby's playlist.
  - `lib/hooks/useMatchMusic(phase)` is called by `/lobby`'s `BrokeredLobby`, which stays mounted
    across the Lobby Screen and the game. It passes each new phase on and, on unmount, hands the
    music back with `setMusicPhase(null)`.
  - A `?track=` playtest owns its own socket, so the game passes its phases on and hands the music
    back on teardown.
  - Free roam and every other Screen change nothing: the Lobby's playlist just plays on.
- **Tests:**
  - `music.test.ts`: tracks shipped and credited, the phase table, shuffle, the graph, the
    crossfade and deferred pause, the duck, next track on end, pause at 0, an autoplay retry, and
    stop then restart.
  - `startAppMusic`, `setMusicPhase` and `phase` (in `music.test.ts`): starts on the first
    gesture, only resumes after that, leaves a Match's music alone, gives the Lobby's playlist
    outside a Match, and is silent without Web Audio.
  - `useMatchMusic.test.tsx`: passes on each new phase, and hands the music back on unmount.
