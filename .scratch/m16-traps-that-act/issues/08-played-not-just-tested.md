# 06 — Played, not just tested

**What to build:** The four traps in a real Round, in two browsers, against a
server that is never restarted between them. M6 and M6.1 both shipped a
knockdown that could not knock anyone down because their verification tickets
sat unfinished — this one is the milestone, not its epilogue.

**Blocked by:** 02, 03, 04, 05

**Status:** planned

- [ ] A Track carrying all four, published the ordinary way, played by two Accounts
- [ ] The sweeper: its arms sweep, its base does not; being hit by an arm does what
      an M11 spinner does; riding the deck works. Whether arms at y 0.38–1.32 are
      fair against a 1.7 m bean is the thing to judge here
- [ ] The trap door: the shudder reads as a warning; the drop happens when the hole
      does, not a beat later; no one is flung by a closing leaf
- [ ] The fragile floor: three arrivals break it on both clients at the same moment;
      breaking it under someone else looks right from both sides; the return delay
      is long enough to be a decision and short enough not to strand anyone
- [ ] The shooter: the ball goes where the barrel pointed; a hit knocks down; spent
      balls on the deck are fun rather than noise; nothing hitches when several are
      alive
- [ ] The three-arm sweeper: whether a jumpable low arm, a timed middle one and
      an overhead high one read at a glance; whether a Ramp that turns the middle
      arm from a shove into a knockdown mid-Round feels fair; the RAMP card and
      the PART pick in the builder
- [ ] The numbers all four default to, judged by playing and moved in `tuning/`

## Notes

- Every visual and feel check in this milestone is the user's — no shader here has
  ever been rasterised in this repo, and the feel numbers are theirs to move.
