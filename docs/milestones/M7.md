# M7 — A Match, not a Round

## Done when

> **Several Rounds run back to back without returning to the Lobby, each one pays Score by
> placement, and the Match ends with a winner on the Standings.**

M6 was explicitly reordered ahead of this ("Reordered ahead of multi-Round advancement", M6.md), on
the reasoning that Survival is only as good as the shoving it enables. That is now built and
verified. This is the deferred half.

**The glossary describes a game that does not exist.** `CONTEXT.md` has always defined a **Match**
as "one full session from Lobby to a single winner, made of several Rounds", and a **Round** as
ending with "whoever Qualified advances to the next Round". Neither is true of the code: M4 runs one
Round and hands control to the host, who clicks a button to send everyone back to the Lobby. There
are Rounds; there is no Match. That gap is the most expensive kind of debt this project carries,
because the whole team already talks as though the thing exists.

**And it is not built the way the glossary said.** A grilling session (2026-09) settled that
elimination stops being a property of the Match and becomes a property of a Round: nobody is knocked
out, every Round pays **Score**, and the highest total after a fixed number of Rounds wins.
Recorded as **ADR 0049**, which also records why Fall Guys' own structure — the reference for match
structure since M1 — is the wrong shape at this project's actual player counts. Two Players cannot
be halved three times.

**Why this and not content.** Nothing else can be judged until Rounds run in sequence. Survival with
a Survivor Target of 1 is a formality for two Players; Power-ups have nothing to balance inside a
single Round; and Track variety does not matter until Tracks follow one another. This is also the
only item on the roadmap that closes a contradiction rather than adding a feature.

## Design

Settled in the grilling session, in full:

- **Placement pays, not raw time.** `buildResults` already ranks a Round — Qualified by finish tick,
  the rest by progress. That placement converts to Score by
  `(1 − (placement−1)/(N−1)) × MAX` over the `N` Players in that Round, so first always takes `MAX`
  and last always takes zero however many are playing. A flat **Qualification bonus** on top.
- **Qualification survives, redefined** — the top scoring tier, not a gate.
- **Match length is fixed**, set in the Lobby, default 3.
- **The host may pick each Round's Track and type, or not.** Whatever is left unpicked the server
  draws, respecting the Round-type/Track compatibility rule M5 ticket 07 already enforces, and never
  repeating a Track until the pool is exhausted.
- **A Standings Screen sits between Rounds** and advances into the next Countdown on its own once
  the next Track has loaded. The same Screen ends the Match — winner named, host returns everyone to
  the Lobby. `returnToLobby` stops being a Round-end action.
- **A tie on Score is broken by the last Round.**
- **The last Round is otherwise ordinary.** `Final Race` and `Skyfall` stay glossary concepts.
- **Out of a Round means Spectator Mode**, following a Player still in it, switchable.
- **A disconnect parks its Score** and takes zero for the Rounds it misses. Joining mid-Match means
  spectating until the next one.
- **Score is derived, not stored.** The server replicates the list of completed Round results; Score
  is a pure function of it in `packages/shared`, the same discipline as `buildResults` and
  `qualificationPlacement`.

## Checklist

### Retire the duplicate fixed-step loop (ticket 01)
- [ ] `advanceFixed` / `FixedSimulation` and `PredictionLoop`'s own accumulator stop being two
      implementations of one timing semantics, with two copies of `EPSILON_MS`
- [ ] Decided binarily, per `docs/architecture-review.md` finding 4 — no third "compatible" variant

### A Survival Round can be ranked (ticket 02)
- [ ] The tick a Character was eliminated is recorded and replicated — `eliminated` is a boolean
      today, and non-Qualified Characters rank by Checkpoint progress, which on the arena Module
      orders nobody
- [ ] `buildResults` ranks the non-Qualified by it, latest elimination first, falling back to
      progress where there is no elimination tick

### Round results and Score (ticket 03)
- [ ] A Round produces a replicable result — who placed where, and who Qualified
- [ ] Score is a pure function of the list of results so far, in `packages/shared`, testable without
      a server or a Rapier world
- [ ] Percentile-normalised and field-size independent: a Round played by four pays the same
      currency as one played by six

### The Match runs several Rounds (ticket 04)
- [ ] A Match runs its configured number of Rounds without passing through the Lobby
- [ ] The completed Round results ride the snapshot, alongside `lobby` and `dnf`
- [ ] `returnToLobby` becomes a Match-end action

### Pick or shuffle (ticket 05)
- [ ] The Lobby sets Match length
- [ ] Each Round's Track and Round type may be picked by the host or left to the server
- [ ] The server's draw respects Round-type/Track compatibility and does not repeat a Track until
      the pool is exhausted

### The Standings Screen (ticket 06)
- [ ] Shows the Round just played and every Player's running Score
- [ ] Between Rounds it advances into the next Countdown itself, once the next Track has loaded
- [ ] At the end of a Match it names the winner and waits for the host

### Spectator Mode (ticket 07)
- [ ] A Player out of a Round follows one still in it, and can switch
- [ ] Ends with the Round, never later

### A disconnect does not corrupt the standings (ticket 08)
- [ ] A Player who drops keeps their Score in the standings and takes zero for the Rounds they miss
- [ ] Someone who connects mid-Match spectates and plays from the next Match
- [ ] `reclaim` is left exactly as it is — unimplemented (ADR 0024)

## How this milestone is verified

**There is no live-verification ticket.** M6 ticket 06 and M6.1 ticket 03 were both written that way
and both sat unfinished while the bugs they existed to catch reached the player instead — including
one (`Ragdoll.activate` applying an impulse to a body Rapier had not yet given a mass, so a
full-charge Hit knocked nobody down) that 1072 unit tests missed. Every ticket here carries its own
live verification in its own "Done when", and is not done without it.

## Explicitly NOT in M7

Real reconnection — `reclaim` stays unimplemented (ADR 0024) · **Bet** and Spectator wagering ·
XP, coins, accounts, or anything that persists across Matches · `Final Race` and **Skyfall** as
mechanics · Power-ups and Item Boxes · collapsing terrain · level themes · new Round types (`Collect
Round`, `Team Round`) · new Modules or Tracks · the rest of `docs/architecture-review.md` (findings
1, 2 and 3 — the `shared` export surface, splitting `game/` into `sim`/`render`, and linting the
dependency direction), which the review's own sequencing puts after finding 4 anyway and which
divides more cleanly once the Match flow has settled.
