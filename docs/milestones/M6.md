# M6 — Hit and Grab, and a real remote Character

## Done when

> **Two Characters can Hit and Grab each other, and every Character but your own is rendered
> as a real, oriented, animated body — never a capsule.**

Reordered ahead of multi-Round advancement (a grilling session, 2026-09): Survival (M5) is
only as good as the shoving it enables, and M5's own scope note named exactly this pairing —
"new mechanics such as Hit or Grab" — as the natural next step once two Round types existed to
carry them. Bundled with the remote-render work because the two reinforce each other: landing
a Hit on a capsule with no front or back reads as a bug, not chaos.

Design settled in a grilling session and recorded as **ADR 0045** (Character facing becomes a
real, replicated field) and **ADR 0046** (remote Characters render the real model instead of
the placeholder capsule that has stood in since M2 ticket 04). `CONTEXT.md` gained `Hit` and a
sharpened `Grab`.

## Checklist

### Facing (protocol)
- [ ] `facing` (yaw) added to `SimInputs`/`CharacterSnapshot`, sent every Tick from the owning
      client's own look-yaw (ADR 0045)
- [ ] Hit's and Grab's own targeting ("the Character just ahead of you") reads `facing`, not a
      direction derived from `velocity`

### A real remote Character
- [ ] Remote Characters render a cloned MushroomKing rig with a per-player color tint,
      replacing `remoteGeometry`/`remoteMaterial`'s capsule (ADR 0046)
- [ ] Remote animation state (Idle/Walk/Run/Jump/Dash) is driven from the `velocity`,
      `grounded`, `dashing`/`dashSpeed` already on `CharacterSnapshot` — no new wire fields
      beyond `facing`
- [ ] Remote Ragdoll/GettingUp reuses the same canned Death-clip collapse the local Character
      already plays, not true bone-driven puppetry (out of scope, ADR 0046)
- [ ] Live-verified with 12 connected Characters (ADR 0011's own ceiling) — frame rate holds
      with 12 cloned rigs + mixers on screen at once

### Hit
- [ ] A short-range melee move, on its own cooldown like Dash, using Bump's existing
      contact-detection pipeline rather than a new hitbox
- [ ] Connecting lands an Impact on the target (Stagger or Ragdoll, by the existing
      magnitude thresholds)
- [ ] Connecting cancels an in-progress Dash for both the striker and the target

### Grab (Hold)
- [ ] Latching onto the Character just ahead, exactly as `CONTEXT.md` already defines Grab
- [ ] The grabber cannot run while holding; both Characters move at a greatly reduced pace for
      the duration
- [ ] The held Character struggles free by moving away from the grabber; otherwise released
      once the grabber's own hold limit runs out
- [ ] Connecting cancels an in-progress Dash for both Characters, the same as Hit
- [ ] Cooldown after release, mirroring Dash's own idiom

### A ragdoll that holds its shape
- [x] The ragdoll's joints gain limits — elbows and knees hinge one way, spine and neck are
      bounded — so a knocked-down Character settles as a body instead of folding into a lump
      (measured today: pelvis→head collapses from 0.75 to 0.07 under gravity alone) (ticket 05)
- [x] Ragdoll bones collide with each other, retiring `collisionGroups.ts`'s "no self-collision
      for M2"; adjacent jointed pairs keep contacts off (ticket 05)
- [x] Rendering is untouched: the canned Death clip still draws the collapse. This fixes the
      simulation, not the puppetry, which stays out of M6 (see below)

### Round-type parity
- [ ] Hit and Grab behave identically in Race and Survival — no Round-type branching, matching
      how Bump already works in both

### Verification
- [ ] Live with two browsers: Hit landing and cancelling an active Dash on both sides; Grab
      latching, both Characters slowed, the held Character struggling free, and the
      hold-limit release path
- [ ] Live with 12 connected Characters for the remote-render perf check above
- [ ] Live with two browsers: a Character knocked down settles as a body on both sides, and the
      two sides agree — the ragdoll runs in client prediction and server authority alike

## Explicitly NOT in M6

The Punch/Boomerang/Shield/etc. Power-up system and Item Box (Hit is a standalone core-kit
move, not a pull-forward of the Power-up of the same name) · a distinct or second remote
model (the same MushroomKing rig, cloned and tinted, not a new asset) · true bone-driven
ragdoll puppet rendering for remote Characters · Round-type-specific tuning of Hit or Grab ·
multi-Round advancement · collapsing terrain · Betting/Spectator · Skyfall.

Note the ragdoll section above is not a softening of "true bone-driven ragdoll puppet rendering":
that stays out of M6. Ticket 05 changes what the physics *does*, never what draws it.

It did, however, remove the reason the puppetry was refused. Bone-driven rendering was tried during
M6 and rejected on sight because the ragdoll folded into a lump; ADR 0047 fixed that, so the
question is open again and **M6.1** takes it up, along with the other thing playing M6 exposed — a
Hit that cannot knock anyone down. Both are deliberate reversals of deferrals made here, recorded
rather than quietly done.
