# M11 — Moving Segments

## Done when

> **A Track author makes any placed piece spin, swing or slide in the builder,
> sees where it will knock players down, and players ride and get hit by it
> identically on every client.**

The asset packs brought hammers, saws, pendulums and spiked plates; only
motion makes them fun. Settled with the user (2026-09-14) and recorded as
**ADR 0061**, with **Motion**, **Ride** and **Spiked** added to `CONTEXT.md`.

## Design

- Motion lives on the Segment: Spin (constant), Swing and Slide (period,
  easing, end pauses, phase), composed spin → swing → slide.
- Pose is a pure function of the Tick in `packages/shared` — never replicated,
  identical in server, client prediction and the builder preview.
- A moving Segment is one kinematic body (ADR 0050 amended for trimeshes).
- Riding carries a grounded Character; leaving keeps the body's velocity.
- Being hit is the existing Impact rule on the closing speed at the contact
  point; `spiked` Assets always knock down.
- The builder plays Motion live and tints surfaces by the Impact they deal.
