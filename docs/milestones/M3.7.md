# M3.7 — Impulses and air

## Done when

> **The Track can push back: pads that speed you up or slow you down, surfaces that launch you, and
> air that carries you.**

M3.6 makes the ground physical; M3.7 makes it act. Everything in this milestone is a one-shot,
latched effect or a force applied from outside the Character — which is exactly why it is a separate
milestone: these are the only mechanics in the surfaces work that touch the replication protocol,
and they land together so it changes once.

Design settled in the same grilling session as M3.6 (2026-09). ADR 0035 (Epoch-latched velocity
writes), ADR 0036 (Volume as its own entity kind), ADR 0037 (speed-gated wall Impact).

## Checklist

### Pads
- [ ] Speed pad and slow pad: an Epoch-latched velocity write plus a temporarily raised (or lowered)
      speed cap that fades — SuperTuxKart's zipper model. A pure continuous multiplier was tried on
      paper and rejected: on a short pad it does almost nothing, because the next tick's cap clips it
- [ ] The latch reuses the existing `Epoch` idiom (`ragdollEpoch`, `respawnCount`), not a new
      mechanism — a wide pad touched over several ticks fires once

### Launch
- [ ] Bounce Surface and launch pad: velocity **set**, not added — idempotent under prediction
      replay, following Quake 3's jump pad, whose launch velocity is precomputed and whose code lives
      in the shared module both sides run
- [ ] Landing after a launch stays harmless (ADR 0037 — no fall damage exists and none is added).
      What hurts is what you hit
- [ ] Wall Impact re-expressed as a **speed** threshold rather than "are you dashing", with magnitude
      scaling with closing speed — so being fired into a wall by a bounce pad finally does something

### Air
- [ ] `Module.volumes?: VolumeConfig[]` — a Volume is its own entity kind, reusing the
      `OrientedBox` → `orientBox` → `pointInOrientedBox` containment pipeline `Checkpoint` already
      proves works on rotated and tilted Segments (ADR 0036)
- [ ] An updraft Volume applies an upward force to a Character inside it. **No flight mode**: no new
      Character state, no new controls, no camera change — you are blown upward and you flail
- [ ] Overlapping Volumes: exactly one wins by priority, never summed — summed forces turn an
      authoring mistake into what looks like a physics bug
- [ ] Every Volume has a maximum induced speed; a wind Volume without one is an unbounded integrator
- [ ] One demo Module per mechanic, visually identical to existing pieces

## Explicitly NOT in M3.7

Water and buoyancy (cut in the grilling session — a liquid Volume is a swimming mechanic wearing a
wind costume) · Low-gravity Volumes, glide, and anything that adds a flight or swim *state* ·
Conveyor belts (ADR 0036) · Horizontal wind, unless an updraft turns out to be trivially
generalisable · **Any visual work**, same as M3.6 · Round rules about shortcuts: a launch pad that
skips half the Track is a deliberate outcome, and policing it needs a concept of finishing a Round,
which arrives in M4 · Teaching the random Track generator to place any of this.
